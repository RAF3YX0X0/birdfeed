Is the content appropriate for a medical setting?

Yes. Posts are calm, educational and never graphic, written to your guidance, and you approve every one before it’s published.

Can you use our real patient photos?

Only with the patient’s written consent, which your practice collects. Without it, we use your team, your practice and designed educational posts.

Do social ads actually book dental appointments?

They can, when they lead to a clear offer and an easy way to book. With tracking set up, you see the calls and booking requests that came from your ads, not just clicks.
