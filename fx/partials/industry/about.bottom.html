What we believe

The principles behind the work.

01

Access is the product

Great marketing shouldn’t require a $5k/mo budget. Every product and pricing decision runs through one filter: does this make it more accessible?

02

Honest over impressive

If something isn’t working, we say so and fix it. If a channel is wrong for your business, we tell you. Transparency is the baseline.

03

Consistency is the mechanism

Marketing works through sustained presence, not bursts. Our infrastructure is built around repeatability and compounding, not one-off impact.

04

People are the product

Every piece of work is produced by a real specialist. Technology helps them work faster, it doesn’t replace the judgment or craft that makes it work.

How much does MadMarketing cost?

Our local SEO plans are priced per month: MAD Local is $199 a month, MAD Local Growth is $449 a month, MAD Search Authority is $599 a month. Everything beyond local SEO, from paid ads and social media to websites and automation, is quoted to your scope as MAD Full Growth. Eligible local businesses can start with the free 30-day challenge.

Is the content made by AI?

No. Every post, video, and caption is made by a real human creative. We only use AI to brainstorm ideas at the very start.

What services do you offer?

Six families of services: MAD Search (Local SEO, Google Business Profile / Google Maps SEO, Citation Management, Technical & On-Page SEO, Content SEO, E-commerce SEO); MAD AI Visibility (Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), Structured Data, AI Visibility Audits); MAD Performance (Google Ads, Meta Ads, Additional Paid Channels, Measurement & Retargeting); MAD Social & Creative (Social Media Management, Creative Production, Branding, UGC & Influencer Support); MAD Web & Conversion (Website Development, Landing Pages, Conversion Rate Optimization, Website Maintenance); MAD Automation (CRM Setup, Lead Follow-Up, AI-Assisted Workflows, Reporting Automation). Start with the one you need and add more when it makes business sense.

Which social channels do you support?

We set your channels in your channel strategy, from Instagram, Facebook and TikTok to LinkedIn, YouTube and your Google Business Profile.

Can I combine multiple services?

Yes, that’s the point. Run social, ads, and SEO together under one dedicated team and one bill, so your channels reinforce each other instead of working in silos.

Is there a contract?

Each monthly plan starts with a written agreement that sets out billing, cancellation, service levels and deliverables, so you know the terms before you start. The free 30-day challenge never turns into a paid subscription without your explicit approval.

What if I don't like the content?

You review the work before anything goes live, and your feedback goes into revisions. What each plan includes is set out in your written agreement.

How do I get started, and how fast will I see content?

Book a 30-minute call, or, if you’re a local business with a verified Google Business Profile, apply for the free 30-day challenge. After the call we send you a scope and a quote, and once you approve it in writing, we start onboarding and agree the timeline for your first deliverables with you.

How do I communicate with my team?

With your account team, by email and on calls. Your agreement sets out who does what and how often we report to you.

What is your refund policy?

Billing, cancellation and refunds are set out in your written agreement before you pay anything. The free 30-day challenge has no agency fee and needs no credit card.

Who is MadMarketing, and where are you based?

MadMarketing is headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri, and serves businesses across the US. Every marketer and creative on your account is vetted by our team.
