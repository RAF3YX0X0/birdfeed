Why real estate agents struggle

What makes social hard for real estate agents.

  1. 01

    Inconsistency reads as inactivity

    A feed that stops at your last closing tells sellers you’re not busy, even when you are.

  2. 02

    Listings outlive the post

    One rushed “just listed” graphic isn’t a marketing plan, every listing deserves a content cycle.

  3. 03

    Personal brand takes time you bill elsewhere

    Market updates and neighborhood content win sellers, but writing them eats selling hours.
