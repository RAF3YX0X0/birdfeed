Done right

The rules of restaurants.

The sensitivities that come with your industry, built into how we plan, write and post. We follow your guidance and your professional body’s rules.

Allergens and dietary claims are exact

We only call a dish vegan, gluten-free or nut-free when you confirm it, and flag shared-kitchen risks the way you want.

Alcohol is promoted responsibly

Drink posts follow the platforms’ alcohol policies and your local rules: aimed at adults, never at excess.

Guests’ photos with permission

Guest photos and tags are shared only with their OK, and credited.

Prices and specials stay current

Specials are dated and prices are real, so nobody turns up for an offer that ended.
