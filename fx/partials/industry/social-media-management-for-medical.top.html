A month on your feed.

What a month of posts could look like for a practice like yours. We plan yours around your offers, your seasons and your goals; your plan sets how many posts you get.

Week 1

  1. MonReel

    Meet your care team

    Patients choose clinicians they feel they know.

  2. WedCarousel

    Doctor, urgent care or ER: which one?

    Practical guidance people save and share.

  3. FriStory

    Appointment availability this week

    A simple, direct way to book.

Week 2

  1. MonReel

    What to expect at your first visit

    Lowers the barrier to booking.

  2. WedCarousel

    A seasonal health tip from our clinicians

    Timely, useful and on-brand.

  3. FriPhoto

    A patient story (with written consent)

    Real experiences, shared the right way.

Week 3

  1. MonReel

    Myth vs. fact on a common condition

    Clear, trustworthy education.

  2. WedCarousel

    Insurance and payment questions

    Answers what stops people booking.

    Ask us: general health questions

    Starts conversations, routed to a visit.

Week 4

  1. MonReel

    A tour of the clinic

    Shows a calm, modern space.

  2. WedCarousel

    This month’s health awareness theme

    Ties your expertise to what people are hearing about.

    A community health event
