Why dental practices struggle

What makes social hard for dental practices.

  1. 01

    An empty feed costs trust

    New patients compare 2–3 practices online; a dormant profile quietly loses the tie-break.

  2. 02

    Clinical content is hard to make friendly

    Procedure posts sound scary or salesy without a writer who knows the tone.

  3. 03

    The front desk has no time for Instagram

    Social falls to whoever is least busy, which means it doesn’t happen.

An example month

A month on your feed.

What a month of posts could look like for a practice like yours. We plan yours around your offers, your seasons and your goals; your plan sets how many posts you get.

Week 1

  1. MonReel

    Meet the team: who you’ll see at your visit

    Familiar faces take the nerves out of booking.

  2. WedCarousel

    Five signs you’re brushing too hard

    Useful tips get saved and shared with family.

  3. FriStory

    Open check-up slots this week

    A direct, low-effort nudge to book.

Week 2

  1. MonReel

    Your first visit, step by step

    Answers “what will happen?” before they ask.

  2. WedPhoto

    A patient’s smile story (with written consent)

    Real outcomes, shared the right way.

  3. FriCarousel

    Myth or fact: whitening

    Clears up confusion and shows expertise.

Week 3

  1. MonReel

    Tips for your child’s first dental visit

    Speaks to the parent who books for the family.

  2. WedCarousel

    Insurance and payment options, explained

    Removes the most common reason people wait.

  3. FriStory

    Ask the dentist: one question answered

    Opens a conversation that often ends in a booking.

Week 4

  1. MonReel

    Inside the practice: tech that makes visits easier

    Shows a modern, comfortable practice.

  2. WedPhoto

    Team milestone or community event

    Local practices win on being part of the community.

  3. FriCarousel

    Thank you: this month’s reviews

    Reviews are what new patients look for first.
