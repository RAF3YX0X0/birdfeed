An example month

A month on your feed.

What a month of posts could look like for a gym like yours. We plan yours around your offers, your seasons and your goals; your plan sets how many posts you get.

Week 1

  1. MonReel

    Monday’s class in 20 seconds

    Lets people picture themselves in the room.

  2. WedCarousel

    Form fix: one move, three cues

    The posts members save and send to friends.

  3. FriStory

    Weekend schedule and free-trial link

    Catches people making weekend plans.

Week 2

  1. MonReel

    Member spotlight (with their OK)

    Real members are more convincing than models.

  2. WedCarousel

    Your first week here: a beginner’s guide

    Answers the fears that stop people joining.

  3. FriPhoto

    Coach intro: who’ll train you

    People join coaches, not just gyms.

Week 3

  1. MonReel

    A workout to do at home this weekend

    Useful content that keeps you in their feed.

  2. WedCarousel

    Nutrition basics from our coaches

    Shows expertise beyond the gym floor.

  3. FriStory

    Poll: which new class should we add?

    Members feel heard; you learn what they want.

Week 4

  1. MonReel

    Challenge or event highlights

    Community is what keeps members paying.

  2. WedPhoto

    Milestones: 100 classes, new PRs

    Celebrates members and motivates everyone else.

  3. FriCarousel

    Memberships and prices, explained

    Answers the question that comes before signing up.
