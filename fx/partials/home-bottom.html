01How much does MadMarketing cost?

It depends on the services you pick. Social media management starts at $99/mo for 10 custom-branded posts on the channels you choose. Other starting prices: short-form videos and email design from $149/mo, SEO blog posts and ad creative from $99/mo, and fully managed Meta or Google Ads at $499/mo. Every service includes a dedicated account manager, an onboarding call, and a monthly content review call, with one set of social channels at no extra cost. Most clients combine two to four services and spend $300 to $600 a month.

02Is the content made by AI?

No. Every deliverable is created by a real person: a graphic designer designs your posts, a copywriter writes your copy, an editor cuts your videos, and a certified media buyer runs your ads. Our team uses modern tools to move faster, but a human creates and quality-checks everything before it reaches you.

03What services do you offer?

More than 15, all available on their own or combined: Social Media Posts, Short-Form Videos, Static and Video Ad Creatives, UGC Videos, Email Marketing, SEO Blog Posts, SEO Backlinks, Managed SEO, Meta Ads Management, Google Ads Management, Instagram Growth, Landing Page Design, Conversion Tracking, Instagram Stories, and Carousel Posts. Pick one, or bundle as many as you need.

04Which social channels do you support?

Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, and Google Business Profile. One account on each channel is included in your plan at no extra cost, and you connect whichever platforms you want through our scheduling dashboard during onboarding.

05Can I combine multiple services?

Yes, and it's how most clients use MadMarketing. Add any mix at checkout, whether that's social posts plus short-form video plus SEO blogs, or ad creative plus Meta Ads management. You can add or remove services anytime from your dashboard, and most clients run two to four services together.

06Is there a contract?

No. Every service is month-to-month. Add, remove, upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime from your dashboard, with no emails, no phone calls, and no cancellation fees. We earn your business every month.

07What if I don't like the content?

You review every deliverable in your dashboard before anything goes live, so nothing publishes without your sign-off. Leave feedback on anything you want changed and your team revises it. Your first month includes up to three revision rounds, then one a month after that, and your first month is free.

08How do I get started, and how fast will I see content?

Pick your services and check out in about two minutes. You fill a short brand questionnaire, join a live onboarding call with your account manager, and connect your channels. Your first deliverables arrive within 10 business days of submitting the questionnaire (a few services with extra steps, like SEO Backlinks and UGC Videos, take a little longer). Once you approve, we schedule and post everything for you. Prefer to talk first? Book a free 30-minute demo.

09How do I communicate with my team?

You get a dedicated account manager who typically replies several times a day and always within 24 hours, plus an onboarding call and a monthly content review call with your content strategist, and you can book a call between meetings anytime. Day-to-day communication happens in your dashboard or by email, so every brief and piece of feedback stays in one place and you never have to repeat yourself.

10What is your refund policy?

Creative services, social posts, short-form videos, blog posts, email design, and static and video ads, come with a 14-day satisfaction guarantee on your first month: work through revisions, and if it still misses, we refund it in full, as long as you haven't approved or scheduled any of that batch. Services with significant upfront or third-party costs (Meta Ads, Google Ads, Managed SEO, SEO Backlinks, UGC Videos, and Instagram Growth) aren't covered, but you can still cancel anytime. Cancellation stops future charges; if you sign up but never onboard, your payment becomes credit that never expires.

11Who is MadMarketing, and where are you based?

MadMarketing is headquartered in St. Louis, Missouri, and serves businesses across the US. Every marketer and creative on your account is vetted by our team.
