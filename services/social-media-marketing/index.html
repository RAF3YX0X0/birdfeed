Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Which platforms do you manage?

That’s set in your channel strategy. We plan the channels, the content calendar and the posting workflow with you.

Do you make the content as well?

Yes. Creative production covers graphics, carousels, short-form reels, ad creatives and video edits, with messaging that fits your brand.

Can you work with creators and influencers?

Yes. We write creator briefs, support sourcing and coordinate campaigns. Creator fees are a separate budget.

How is social media priced?

It’s quoted to your scope as part of MAD Full Growth, with defined deliverables and a reporting cadence. On-site filming and high-volume creative production are scoped separately.
