Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the work of improving how a business appears in location-based searches, such as “near me” searches and Google Maps, through its Google Business Profile, listings, website and reviews.

How much does local SEO cost?

MAD Local is $199 a month, MAD Local Growth is $449 a month, MAD Search Authority is $599 a month. Each plan starts with a written agreement that sets out billing, cancellation and deliverables.

How long does local SEO take?

It depends on your market, your competition and where you’re starting from. We set expectations after an audit, and report what was done and what changed along the way.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Search placement is determined by search engines and varies by market, competition, relevance, and other factors. We report what was done, what changed, and what comes next.

Do I need a physical storefront?

No. Service-area businesses that visit customers can also have a Google Business Profile and benefit from local SEO.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. Eligible businesses can start with the MAD 30-Day Visibility Challenge, a defined local SEO scope for no agency fee. It never turns into a paid subscription without your approval.
