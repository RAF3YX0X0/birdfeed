Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What is AI search optimization?

It’s the work of structuring your content and business information so AI-powered search experiences can understand and accurately describe your expertise.

Is this different from SEO?

It builds on the same foundations (useful content, clear structure, accurate information) with extra attention to how answers are summarized.

Can you guarantee my business appears in AI answers?

No. AI-powered search experiences decide what they show, and their answers vary. We focus on the parts you can influence and report what we did.
