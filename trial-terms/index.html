The MAD 30-Day Visibility Challenge.

Last updated October 13, 2026

The offer

The MAD 30-Day Visibility Challenge (the “trial”) is a defined 30-day scope of local SEO work that MADMarketing provides to accepted businesses for $0 in agency fees.

The trial includes: a Google Business Profile audit and optimization; local SEO and competitor analysis; up to 10 local citation actions (submissions or corrections); baseline and follow-up local search visibility snapshots using comparable searches, locations and reporting methods; review growth recommendations; and a personalized 90-day growth roadmap.

Price and payment

There is no agency fee for the trial and no credit card is required. The trial does not renew automatically into a paid plan.

Any optional third-party paid tools or services must be approved by you in advance and are paid for separately by your business.

Eligibility and acceptance

The trial is available to legitimate local storefront or service-area businesses in markets we support, applying through an owner or authorized decision-maker.

An existing verified Google Business Profile is required before the trial is activated, and manager access to it is required before work begins. A website is preferred but not required. Suspended profiles are reviewed individually.

Every application is reviewed for business legitimacy, profile status, location, eligibility, scope and team capacity. Admission is subject to available slots, and we may decline any application. One trial per business or location.

Access and ownership

If you’re accepted, you grant MADMarketing appropriate manager access to your Google Business Profile. You keep ownership of your profile and can remove our access at any time. Never share your Google password with us or anyone else.

No guarantees

We guarantee the agreed work and transparent reporting, not a specific outcome. Google ranking positions, traffic increases and new leads are not guaranteed. Results vary by market, competition, relevance and other factors, and may take weeks or months.

Directory publication and indexing are controlled by third parties, so we can’t guarantee that a submitted or corrected listing will be accepted, published or indexed.

Ethical practices

We follow legitimate review practices. We do not create fake reviews or manipulated engagement.

Timeline and what happens next

The trial runs for 30 days from activation. At the end you receive a report covering the work completed, citation activity, visibility observations and recommended next steps, together with an optional proposal.

Continuing is your choice. If you don’t subscribe to an ongoing plan, the trial simply ends, without any automatic charge. Ongoing packages and scopes are confirmed in a separate proposal.

Your information

We use the details in your application to review it and to contact you about it. See our privacy policy for how we handle personal information.
