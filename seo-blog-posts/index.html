An AI tool can write a blog post in 60 seconds. That is not the problem.

The problem is writing posts that rank for keywords nobody searches with buying intent, building a blog that gets readers but no customers. Strategy is what separates a content library from a revenue channel.

The common approach

Write posts. Hope they rank. Wonder why nothing converts.

✕Pick topics that feel relevant, with no data on whether anyone is actually searching for them
✕Target informational keywords ("what is X") that attract curious readers who never buy
✕Publish random posts with no content cluster strategy, so nothing builds authority for anything
✕Use an AI tool and publish the output directly, producing generic content 200 other sites already have
✕Measure success by traffic, not by the leads or revenue generated
The MadMarketing approach

Start with the keywords your buyers actually use. Build around them.

✓Keyword research first, every month, targeting what people search when they are ready to spend money
✓Content clusters built around your core services, so your site builds authority where it drives revenue
✓Long-form, well-researched posts that go deeper than the competition, the kind Google rewards with first-page rankings
✓Written by real people with knowledge of your industry, with original angles, cited sources, and a point of view
✓Internal linking strategy built in, so every post feeds authority to the pages that matter most
Commercial intent

We target the searches people make right before they buy.

Not the ones they make when they are just curious.

Most blog content targets informational searches. Those bring traffic. MadMarketing targets commercial and transactional keywords, searches like "best [tool] for [use case]" and "[service] near me", that bring buyers.

✓

Best-of and comparison posts

"Best [your product category] for [their situation]" ranks well and converts, because the reader is already in buying mode.

✓

Alternative and vs. posts

"X vs Y" and "[competitor] alternative" searches are often your most valuable traffic, because that person is actively choosing right now.

✓

Problem-aware posts

Posts that meet readers at the exact moment they realize they have a problem your service solves.
