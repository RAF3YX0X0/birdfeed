Google Ads, Capture existing demand

Your customer has a problem. They search for it. Your ad appears at the exact moment of intent. Google doesn’t need to convince anyone they have a problem, it just needs to show them you’re the right solution. This is why Google Search consistently delivers the highest-intent traffic of any ad platform.

Highest purchase intent

Meta Ads, Create demand

Meta shows your ad to people who weren’t thinking about you, and tries to make them care. It works for brand building, retargeting, and broad-appeal products. But it’s a fundamentally different job: you’re interrupting a scroll, not answering a search. Both channels have a place in a full-funnel strategy.

Awareness & consideration
Why most Google Ads fail

Google’s automation is powerful. But only if you feed it the right signals.

In 2026, Google’s smart bidding does the heavy lifting, but the algorithm is only as good as the data you give it. Garbage in, garbage out. If your conversion tracking is broken, your bidding optimizes toward the wrong actions and your budget is wasted on signals that don’t correlate with revenue. The same applies to structure: too many campaigns and overlapping keywords create internal competition. Complexity is the enemy of performance. MadMarketing fixes both: right structure, right tracking, from day one.

What makes the difference

Three things most Google Ads managers get wrong

MadMarketing’s Google Ads service is built around the three variables that actually determine campaign performance in 2026.

01, Signals

Clean conversion data. Not just "conversions tracked."

Smart bidding (tCPA or tROAS) is only as good as the conversion data you feed it. Most accounts track page views or form opens, and the algorithm optimizes toward those, not actual purchases or qualified leads. We set up conversion tracking to measure what actually drives revenue. This single change routinely produces the biggest ROAS improvements of anything we do.

02, Structure

The 2026 Power Pack, not seven campaigns fighting each other.

Google’s recommended structure for 2026 is the "Power Pack": Performance Max for broad reach, AI Max for Search for high-intent queries, and Demand Gen for consideration-stage audiences. Most accounts have none of this, they have 5–10 disconnected campaigns with overlapping keywords. We build the right architecture from the start, or rebuild it if you’re coming from a mess.

03, Copy

Ad copy written by someone who knows what Google is actually testing.

Google’s search ads automatically test combinations of your headlines and descriptions, but only the ones you give it. Most businesses provide generic, similar headlines that give the algorithm nothing interesting to test. We write up to 15 distinct headlines per campaign, then use Google’s performance reports to refresh what isn’t working every month.
