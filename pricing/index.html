Good to know

How we work with you.

Prices are in US dollars

Each plan starts with a written agreement that sets out billing, cancellation, service levels and deliverables.

What a plan doesn’t include

Advertising spend, hosting, paid directory listings, premium software and extensive development are excluded unless they’re written into your agreement.

What needs its own scope

Multi-location work, substantial new page builds, on-site filming, custom integrations and high-volume creative production are scoped and quoted separately.

Your accounts stay yours

You own your business profiles and advertising accounts. We work through role-based access and never ask for your passwords.

No guaranteed outcomes

We don’t guarantee Google positions, AI recommendations, review counts, traffic, ROAS or leads. We guarantee the agreed work and transparent reporting.

The free trial stays free

The 30-day challenge never turns into a paid subscription without your explicit approval.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Which plan should I start with?

MAD Local Growth is the plan we recommend. MAD Local is the entry-level plan for local presence management, and MAD Search Authority adds deeper website and local search optimization.

Is there a contract?

Each monthly plan starts with a written agreement that sets out billing, cancellation, service levels and deliverables, so you know the terms before you start.

Do I have to pay after the free 30 days?

No. The MAD 30-Day Visibility Challenge has no agency fee, needs no credit card and never turns into a paid subscription without your explicit approval.

Is ad spend included?

No. Advertising spend, hosting, paid directory listings, premium software and extensive development are excluded unless they’re written into your agreement.

What does MAD Full Growth cost?

It’s quoted to your scope: a custom combination of SEO, paid ads, social media, website/CRO and automation, with defined deliverables, account responsibilities and a reporting cadence. Media budgets and third-party costs are quoted separately.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Search placement is determined by search engines and varies by market, competition, relevance, and other factors. We report what was done, what changed, and what comes next.

Will you need my passwords?

No. You own your business profiles and advertising accounts. We work through role-based access and never ask for passwords.
