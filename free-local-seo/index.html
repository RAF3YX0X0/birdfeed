FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions.

Still unsure? Email hello@madmediamarketing.com.

Is the trial really free?

Yes. Eligible businesses receive the agreed 30-day Local SEO work for $0 agency fees. No credit card is required. Any optional third-party paid tools or services must be separately approved and paid for by the business in advance.

Will you get my business to #1 on Google?

No. No agency can honestly guarantee a particular Google ranking. We optimize your local search foundations and document progress.

Do I need a website?

A website is helpful but not mandatory for the basic trial. Your business needs to be a legitimate local business and meet our eligibility requirements.

What are local citations?

Business information listings or mentions on relevant websites and directories. Consistent business information helps customers find accurate details and supports a well-maintained local presence.

How long does SEO take?

Results vary and may take weeks or months. The 30-day trial demonstrates real work, establishes a useful baseline, and identifies future opportunities.

What happens after 30 days?

You receive a report and an optional proposal. You can subscribe to an ongoing plan or stop. There is no automatic paid renewal.

Do you need my Google password?

No. Never share your Google password. If you’re approved, you grant us appropriate manager access to your Google Business Profile, and you keep ownership of it.

Will you create reviews or guarantee leads?

No. No fake reviews, manipulated engagement, guaranteed leads or guaranteed ranking positions. We follow legitimate review practices.

Is every business accepted?

No. Applications are screened for business legitimacy, profile status, location, eligibility, scope and team capacity.
