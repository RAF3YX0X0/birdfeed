The MAD 30-Day Visibility Challenge

Your first 30 days are on us.

See what focused local SEO work looks like before you commit to a monthly plan. Eligible businesses receive a defined 30-day scope for no agency fee, followed by an optional proposal.

Apply now Check eligibility

$0 agency fee for eligible businesses. No credit card. No automatic subscription.

What you receive

  • A Google Business Profile audit and optimization
  • Local competitor research
  • Up to 10 citation submissions or corrections
  • Baseline and follow-up visibility snapshots
  • A 90-day roadmap

Eligibility

Who the challenge is for.

We take on a limited number of challenges at a time, so we review every application against these criteria.

You’re eligible if
  • You run a local or service-area business that serves customers in the United States
  • You have a Google Business Profile, or are able to create and verify one
  • You’re the owner, or are authorized to make marketing decisions for the business
  • You can give us manager access to your profile and share basic business details
  • Someone on your side can answer questions and approve changes during the 30 days
The challenge isn’t available for
  • Businesses that already completed a MADMarketing challenge (one per business or location)
  • Current MADMarketing clients on a paid SEO plan
  • Businesses without a real address or service area customers can be served from
  • Categories Google restricts or doesn’t allow on Business Profiles
  • Online-only businesses with no local customers

Limited eligibility and capacity. Meeting the criteria doesn’t guarantee a place; we’ll let you know either way.

Questions

Before you apply.

How does the free trial work?

Eligible businesses receive a defined 30-day local SEO scope for no agency fee, followed by an optional proposal.

Is there a catch?

No credit card and no automatic subscription. At the end of the 30 days you get your results and a 90-day roadmap, and you decide whether to continue.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No. Search placement is determined by search engines and varies by market, competition, relevance, and other factors. We report what was done, what changed, and what comes next.

Why is eligibility limited?

Each challenge is real work from our team, so we take on a limited number at a time and focus on businesses where local SEO is a good fit.
