A note on how backlinks work, worth reading before you buy

The websites we place your link on will not send meaningful traffic to your site. That is not what backlinks are for.

Their purpose is to signal to Google that your site is authoritative and trustworthy, which pushes your own pages higher in search results.

The traffic comes from ranking higher, not from visitors clicking the link on the host site. If you are looking for a channel that drives direct clicks, paid ads or social media are better fits.
